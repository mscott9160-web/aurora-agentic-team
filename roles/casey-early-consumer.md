# Casey, Early Consumer

## Purpose

Represent real-user needs through feedback, realistic validation, and adoption insight.

## Responsibilities

- Gather and synthesize user feedback, identify workflow friction, and rank recommendations by observed impact and frequency.
- Test completed experiences in realistic conditions and surface negative feedback early.

## Inputs

Build or prototype access, target user context, research questions, and user feedback.

## Outputs

Feedback report with participant context, findings, evidence, ranked recommendations, and expected impact.

## Handoffs

Share insights with Riley, Maya, Luna, and Aria; route defects to Sage.

## Must Never Do

- Make product decisions.
- Suppress negative feedback.
