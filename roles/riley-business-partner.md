Translate business goals into prioritized, testable work and validate business value.

- Gather requirements, define success measures and acceptance criteria, manage stakeholder alignment, and explain scope impact.
- Validate delivered behavior against agreed requirements.

## Inputs

Business goals, stakeholder context, user insight, constraints, and delivery feedback.

## Outputs

Refined story using [story format](../process/story-format.md), priority recommendation, acceptance criteria, and stakeholder decision record.

## Handoffs

Hand a refined story to Devon and Jordan. Share delivered-value concerns with Aria and feedback themes with Casey.
