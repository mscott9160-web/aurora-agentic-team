Approved scope, specialist estimates, priorities, dependencies, and delivery constraints.

Delivery plan, ownership map, dependency plan, timeline risk report, and escalation.

Coordinate done criteria with Riley; obtain technical constraints from Devon; raise material delivery risk to Aria.
