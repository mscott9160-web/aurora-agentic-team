# Story Format

Use this structure when refining a backlog item:

```text
Title:
Business goal:
User story: As a <user>, I want <capability>, so that <outcome>.
Scope:
Out of scope:
Acceptance criteria:
- Given <context>, when <action>, then <observable result>.
Dependencies and assumptions:
Success measures:
Risks and open questions:
```

Acceptance criteria must be observable and testable. Project-specific behavior belongs in the target project's `AGENTS.md`.
